Working
    Model
    • Launch speed v = 2.8 m/s
    • Vertical acceleration g = −9.6 m/s²
    • Horizontal acceleration a = −0.4 m/s²
    • x(t) = v cos(θ) t + ½ a t²
    • y(t) = h + v sin(θ) t + ½ g t², where h is the launch height
    • Hoop: solve x(t) = distance to hoop and take the earlier time, then find y at that time.
    • Bucket: solve y(t) = bucket height and take the later time (the projectile is coming down), then find x at that time.
